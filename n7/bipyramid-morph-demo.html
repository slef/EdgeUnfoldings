What is proved, and what remains open?

Every equator vertex has a valid straight-line insertion from a six-vertex hull. Convexity and monotonicity of its curvature hold throughout. The first displayed tree fails for the first example; a different fixed tree succeeds for that entire path. Exact interval certificates cover the successful trees for both examples. None of this proves that every pentagonal bipyramid has such a successful tree.

The next question is how to choose a suitable tree, or repair a first collision by changing a slit or pole.
