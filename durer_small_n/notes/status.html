Edge unfoldings with at most six vertices: proof status

A net is a non-overlapping edge unfolding (touching counts as non-overlapping). This page shows how the theorem splits into combinatorial types, which cases are settled and why, and exactly what remains open for the octahedron. Every figure is a real unfolding computed from a real polytope.

Current position · 9 September 2026. Four of the seven six-vertex types are settled here. The octahedron, octahedron minus an edge, and prism with one diagonal remain open in general.

Latest finding: an exact convex example rules out the individual-apex shortcut, while its whole net is nonoverlapping. The earlier cone proof and certified minus-edge neighborhood remain valid.
