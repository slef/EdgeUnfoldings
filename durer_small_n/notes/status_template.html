Edge unfoldings with at most six vertices: proof status

A net is a non-overlapping edge unfolding (touching counts as non-overlapping). This page shows how the theorem splits into combinatorial types, which cases are settled and why, and exactly what remains open for the octahedron. Every figure is a real unfolding computed from a real polytope.

Morning results, 9 September 2026. The full n=6 case remains unproved here. Direct review found an exact counterexample to the DF entry of DiBiase's displayed octahedron chart. A new base-cone proof handles the large-angle and equality branches. Lemma L and the remaining fan-angle sum below π are open. Independent replay has verified a complete 443-region prism certificate and a larger 409-region octahedron-minus-edge certificate. A new exact example rules out angular separation around w as a complete proof of Lemma L. Global coverage remains open. See the exact coverage bounds and the working notes.
