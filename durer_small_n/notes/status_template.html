Every polytope with at most six vertices has a net: the case tree

A net is a non-overlapping edge unfolding (touching counts as non-overlapping). This page shows how the theorem splits into combinatorial types, which cases are settled and why, and exactly what remains open for the octahedron. Every figure is a real unfolding computed from a real polytope.
